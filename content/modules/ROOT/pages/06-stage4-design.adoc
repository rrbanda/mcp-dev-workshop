= Stage 4: Design
:navtitle: Stage 4: Design

*Duration:* ~5 minutes +
*What happens:* AgentSherpa proposes a tool design for your MCP server and asks you to approve it.

== Design process

Based on your stated intent (Stage 2), AgentSherpa presents:

. A list of proposed *tools* with names, descriptions, and parameters
. The *data source* and how each tool will access it
. The *transport* (Streamable HTTP on port 8080, path `/mcp`)

For the stock market server, the default design includes 9 tools across four categories:

[cols="1,2",options="header"]
|===
| Tool | Purpose

| `get_stock_info`
| Comprehensive stock data — current price, P/E ratio, market cap, 52-week range, company profile

| `get_historical_stock_prices`
| OHLCV price history with configurable period and interval

| `get_stock_news`
| Latest news articles for a ticker from Yahoo Finance

| `get_stock_actions`
| Dividends and stock splits history

| `get_financial_statement`
| Income statement, balance sheet, or cash flow (annual or quarterly)

| `get_holder_info`
| Institutional holders, insider transactions, major holders

| `get_option_expiration_dates`
| Available options expiration dates for a ticker

| `get_option_chain`
| Options chain (calls/puts) with optional strike filtering and field projection

| `get_recommendations`
| Analyst recommendations or upgrades/downgrades history
|===

== Your role

* *Review* the tool list -- do you want all 9, or fewer?
* *Suggest changes* -- rename tools, add parameters, remove tools
* *Approve* the design before the agent starts building

AgentSherpa will not write any code until you approve.
