== Step 9b: Knowledge check

AgentSherpa presents the final quiz with 8 questions covering:

* *Concepts (Q1-Q2):* MCP protocol fundamentals, lifecycle layers
* *Build & Design (Q3-Q6):* Tool definitions, error handling, SDK patterns
* *Deploy & Govern (Q7-Q8):* MCPServer CR, MCP Gateway registration

You need *5 out of 8 correct* to pass. The agent explains incorrect answers.

== Step 9c: Certificate

If you pass the quiz, AgentSherpa generates a personalized workshop certificate showing:

* Your name
* Workshop title: "MCP Developer Workshop on OpenShift"
* Date of completion
* Final score

== Step 9d: Cleanup (optional)

AgentSherpa offers to clean up your deployed resources. Cleanup removes:

. MCP server connection from OpenCode
. MCPServerRegistration (gateway deregistration)
. HTTPRoute
. MCPServer CR (operator garbage-collects the Deployment, Service, NetworkPolicy)
. BuildConfig and ImageStream

The cleanup is optional -- your facilitator may clean up all namespaces after the workshop.

See xref:12-cleanup.adoc[Cleanup] for the detailed cleanup procedure.
